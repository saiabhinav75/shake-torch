# Privacy Policy: Shake Torch

_Last updated: 29 September 2026_

Shake Torch ("the app") lets you toggle your phone's flashlight by shaking the device.

## Data collection

**The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.**

- The app has no internet permission and cannot send data off your device.
- Accelerometer readings are processed in memory, on the device, only to detect a shake. They are
  never recorded or stored.
- The camera is used only to switch the flashlight on and off. No photos or video are captured.
- Your settings (shake sensitivity, cooldown, etc.) are stored locally on your device and are removed
  when you uninstall the app.
- The app contains no ads, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Foreground service | Keep listening for shakes while the app is in the background |
| Notifications | Show the required "listening" notification with torch controls |
| Wake lock | Keep detecting shakes while the screen is off (optional setting) |
| Vibrate | Optional haptic feedback when the torch toggles |
| Run at startup | Resume listening after the phone restarts, if you had it enabled |

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
